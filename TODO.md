## Basketball
- [ ] Complete post play: backdowns, shoulder fakes, drop steps, up-and-unders, hooks and quick fades.
- [ ] Expand contextual finishes: inside hand, wrong foot, protected reverses, scoops, contact finishes and dunk variations.
- [ ] Make every shot and finish have situational advantages from its actual motion: calculate contest from the ball's release position and timing relative to defender hand reach, elevation, angle and movement, including separation and body/rim protection.
- [ ] Calculate shot difficulty separately from defensive contest: balance, momentum, range, release angle and timing can make a less-contested finish harder to convert. Avoid blanket contest discounts based only on a move's name.
- [ ] Compare finishes against the same defender position and jump timing: a fade that creates separation should receive less contest than a pull-up; hooks, reverses, floaters and scoops should benefit only when their release or protection beats that defender. Anticipation and correct positioning must let defenders counter those advantages. Keep visible animation, contest feedback and scoring/block calculations consistent for players and AI.
